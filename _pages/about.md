I am an AI/ML and Medical AI researcher and engineer, currently a **Graduate Trainee Engineer – IT at Osol Tech (Fatima Group)**, where I work on end-to-end deep learning systems from training and quantization to deployment on enterprise infrastructure.

I completed my **B.S. in Data Science at GIKI** (Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute of Engineering Sciences and Technology) in June 2026 with **four consecutive Dean's Honour Lists**. I came to GIKI from a pre-medical background and a 2.5 first-semester GPA; by graduation my final-year project was a system that clones a human voice from just 3 seconds of audio. I build AI systems that *see, hear, and generate* not just analyze data.

Before Osol, I was a **Research Intern at BIOMISA Lab, NUST**, building clinical AI pipelines (Whisper ASR → SOAP notes → ICD-10 extraction with Med-Gemma) and clinical risk models, and an **AI Backend Engineer at Code Knitters**, shipping LLM/GenAI products from model integration and quantization to API deployment.

Research interests
======
* **Speech & generative AI: ** voice cloning, TTS accent adaptation, LLMs, RAG, and agentic systems. My thesis work adapts a 520M-parameter TTS model to South Asian English with LoRA, training only ~0.1% of weights ([manuscript](/publication/2026-06-01-voice-cloning-lora)).
* **Efficient & trustworthy ML: ** model quantization (INT8/INT4), parameter-efficient fine-tuning, model compression, and what these optimizations do to calibration and reliability especially in clinical settings ([ongoing audit](https://github.com/Mustafa-bacha/pcg-quant-audit)).
* **Medical AI: ** clinical documentation pipelines, physiological time-series modeling, risk stratification, and data-driven medical decision making.
* **Computer vision & perception: ** real-time detection and localization on edge hardware, vision-language models, and interpretability.
