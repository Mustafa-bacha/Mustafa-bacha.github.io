**SpeechEcho** delivers natural, high-quality voice cloning and dialogue synthesis tuned for South Asian English — a variety spoken by 500M+ people yet underrepresented in mainstream TTS training data.

* Clones a voice from just a **3-second audio sample**.
* Accent adaptation via **LoRA fine-tuning** of the 520M-parameter Chatterbox TTS (Llama backbone): 210 adapted projections, ~0.1% of weights trained ([manuscript](/publication/2026-06-01-voice-cloning-lora)).
* Showcased at the **GIKI Industrial Open House 2026**; received offers for collaboration and commercialization.
* Team: Muhammad Mustafa Shah, Hussain Ahmad, Saud Khan. Supervisors: Dr. Farah Saeed, Ms. Rabbia Hassan.
