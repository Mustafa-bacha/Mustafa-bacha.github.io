A comprehensive AI research tool that pairs practical retrieval with from-scratch theory:

* **RAG pipeline for ArXiv Q&A** — ask questions about downloaded papers and get answers with citations.
* **Custom transformers built from scratch** (encoder and decoder architectures) and trained for classification, generation, and summarization — demonstrating the internals behind modern LLMs rather than only calling APIs.
* Full-stack: Python backend, Next.js frontend.
