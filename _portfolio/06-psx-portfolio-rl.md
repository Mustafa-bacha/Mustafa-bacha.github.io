A data-driven algorithmic agent for portfolio investment under uncertainty:

* Formulated equity trading as a stochastic **Markov Decision Process**, with deep sequential models (LSTMs) capturing non-linear market dynamics, volatility, and temporal asset correlations.
* Trained with **Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)** to learn allocation policies across volatile market regimes.
* **Results**: +18.4% annually vs the KSE-100 index, Sharpe ratio 1.85, maximum drawdown reduced by 22% during high-volatility periods.
