Built during my research internship at BIOMISA Lab (NUST):

* **End-to-end clinical AI pipeline**: transcribes clinical voice recordings with Whisper ASR, auto-generates structured SOAP notes, and extracts ICD-10 codes using the Med-Gemma medical LLM — improving documentation efficiency by **45–60%**.
* **Clinical risk models** for Physical Frailty Assessment across 50+ biomarker datasets for patient risk stratification.
* Optimized preprocessing with Pandas/NumPy (**40% latency reduction**) and standardized records into **FHIR-compliant formats** with 95% data integrity.
