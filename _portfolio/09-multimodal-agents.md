A large-scale agentic architecture for stochastic, continuous multimodal input:

* Architected as a **Directed Acyclic Graph of specialized agents** (vision, speech, text) in LangGraph, asynchronously processing **50+ hours of streaming input daily** over distributed Kafka pipelines.
* **Retrieval-Augmented Generation with semantic grounding** to suppress hallucinations during cross-modal reasoning and synthesis.
* Maintained **99.9% uptime**, demonstrating the viability of large-scale agentic systems in stochastic operational environments.
