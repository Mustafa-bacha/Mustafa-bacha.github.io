Transforms any YouTube video into something you can read, search, and interrogate:

* **RAG chatbot** (Groq AI) for instantly querying video content with grounded answers.
* **Transcript viewer and intelligent summaries** for rapid review.
* Full-stack TypeScript: Next.js frontend, Express backend, Prisma ORM.
