Presented our final-year project **SpeechEcho** — real-time voice cloning and conversational speech synthesis designed for accurate South Asian (Pakistani/Indian) accents — to industry professionals, researchers, and tech leaders, together with teammates Hussain Ahmad and Saud Khan (supervisors: Dr. Farah Saeed, Ms. Rabbia Hassan).

The system clones a voice from just a **3-second audio sample**. The response was overwhelmingly positive, with valuable feedback and offers for potential collaboration and commercialization. The project aims to make voice AI more inclusive by bridging the accent-representation gap in conversational AI for millions of users in South Asia.
