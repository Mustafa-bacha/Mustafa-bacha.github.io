Parameter-Efficient Voice Cloning for South Asian English via LoRA Fine-Tuning of Chatterbox TTS

M. M. Shah

Zero-shot TTS models fail on underrepresented accents. We curate a novel 577-speaker South Asian English corpus with word-level alignments and adapt a 520M-parameter Llama-backbone TTS system by injecting LoRA adapters into 210 transformer projections — training only ~0.1% of weights on under 40 minutes of audio, with zero added inference cost after merging.
